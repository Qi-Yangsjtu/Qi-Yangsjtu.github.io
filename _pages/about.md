## About Me

I am a **Research Scientist at ByteDance America**, working with [Dr. Kai Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5rhIVxQAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao) and [Dr. Li Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=8G5-2OMAAAAJ).

My research focuses on **efficient 3D visual representations and perceptual quality assessment**. I work on compact 3D Gaussian Splatting generation and compression, quality assessment of 3D Gaussian Splatting, point clouds, and meshes, and immersive media streaming.

I received my Ph.D. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) and my bachelor's degree from [Xidian University](https://en.xidian.edu.cn/).
